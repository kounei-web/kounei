☃︎ 聖誕夜燈 | Christmas Night light

展示影片

製作軟體 Maya | Design  By  MAYA

聖誕節給人的印象是寒冷的 卻是一個讓人讓人感到快樂且溫暖的節日
Christmas is often associated with coldness, yet it is a holiday that brings joy and warmth.
所以我選擇使用夜燈傳達意象
Therefore, I chose to convey this imagery through a night light.
運用3D建模的方式呈現更有實體感 進行打燈、算圖營造夜燈效果
By using 3D modeling, I aimed to create a more tangible representation, incorporating lighting and rendering to evoke the effect of a night light.
